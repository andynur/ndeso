# Cultural review — Balé

Balé is set in a real place: **Kelurahan Baledono, Purworejo, Jawa Tengah**. That is what
gives the game its texture, and it is also how we could get things wrong in a way that
matters to people who actually live there.

This folder holds what a reviewer needs and what we need back. It exists because
[CULTURE_GUIDE §1.5](../CULTURE_GUIDE.md) moved the first review round from M4 up to
**M1-11 — before any M2 dialog is written.** Rewriting a character after their voice
exists is much harder than choosing it correctly.

## Who we are looking for

Not one person, and not experts in games. The useful readers are:

| | Why |
|---|---|
| Someone who lives or grew up in **Baledono or Purworejo town** | The only people who can say whether the place feels right |
| Someone from a **pesantren or NU background in Kedu/Mataraman** | The religious-depiction rules in CULTURE_GUIDE §3 need a check from inside the tradition |
| A **Javanese speaker from the Bagelen dialect area** | Speech level (ngoko/krama) is characterisation; getting it wrong reads as rude |
| A **farmer, or anyone who knows the pranata mangsa in practice** | Whether the calendar is a living thing or a museum piece |

One person can be several of these. Two or three readers is enough for the first round.

## What to send them

1. [`review-pack.md`](review-pack.md) — the whole concept in about ten minutes of reading
2. [`questions.md`](questions.md) — what we actually want to know

Send both. The pack without the questions gets polite approval; the questions without the
pack get guesses.

## What we owe a reviewer

- **Credit, on their terms.** Name, initials, or anonymous — their choice, recorded in
  `assets/CREDITS.md` and the release notes.
- **A real answer.** Every point gets a written response: changed, not changed and why, or
  still open. "Thanks, noted" is not a response.
- **No obligation to be nice.** We are asking for the thing that is hard to say.

## What happens to the answers

Each round lands as a file here, `round-NN-<date>.md`, with the reviewer's points and what
we did about each. Changes that follow become their own PRs referencing it. Points we
decline stay visible in the file — a record of what was considered, not a list of what was
accepted.

Anything that contradicts [CULTURE_GUIDE](../CULTURE_GUIDE.md) wins over the guide, and
the guide gets updated in the same PR.
