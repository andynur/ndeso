# Reference photography shot list (M1-12)

> **Human task** ([PLACES §5](PLACES.md#5-reference-photography-is-the-bottleneck)). Almost
> nothing of Baledono is online at usable quality, and the art direction is blocked on it.
> This is the list; the walking is yours.

## Ground rules

- **Consent first.** Photograph places, not people. Anyone recognisable in frame: ask, or
  retake without them. No children's faces. On private ground (a venue, someone's
  pendopo) ask the owners before shooting at all, and respect a no.
- **Mosques and musholla:** outside only, never during prayer times, never people praying.
  The game shows them as social landmarks, not game spaces (CULTURE_GUIDE), so the
  outside and the gathering space in front are all the art needs.
- **Your own photos only**, and you agree they are reference, not shipped art: they go to
  the art team, not into `assets/`. Don't commit them to the repo (size, and people in the
  background). Put them in a shared folder and link it from the issue.
- Shoot wide *and* close: one frame for the layout, then the textures in it.
- Phone is fine. Turn on location tagging, or note the spot: PLACES §2 compresses the map,
  so *roughly where* matters more than GPS precision.
- Note the time with each set: the lighting keyframes (DESIGN §1.3) are what these check.

## The list

Priority: **A** blocks the first art pass (Balé, M2), **B** blocks the town (M2 hub), **C** is nice to have.

### A kampung balé and its garden (A) — any pendopo or joglo in Central Java will do
- [ ] From four sides, morning (~07:00) and late afternoon (~16:30)
- [ ] At maghrib, 17:15–17:45: the signature hour — the sky, the light on walls, where lamps come on
- [ ] Posts, joints, roof underside and tiles, if there is a joglo or pendopo structure — close-ups
- [ ] Floor/platform material, steps, edges
- [ ] Any kalen or ditch nearby: dry and wet if you can, its walls, where planks or small bridges (*wot*) cross it
- [ ] Fields next to it: bare, planted, overgrown; the edges between field and path
- [ ] Fences (*pagar*): bamboo, hedge, block — whatever is there
- [ ] Trees and plants you'd see from the ground: species if you know them

### Pasar Baledono (B)
- [ ] 06:00, when it is actually busy: the whole scene, from a spot that doesn't block anyone
- [ ] Stall fronts: roofs, tarps, tables, baskets, crates, scales, signage style (no brand logos needed)
- [ ] The floor, drains, the edge where the market meets the road
- [ ] The same spots at 10:00, emptier, for the contrast

### The main road (B)
- [ ] After rain: wet asphalt, puddles, reflections, the colour of the sky
- [ ] At night: street lights, warung lights, what is lit and what is dark
- [ ] Warung fronts: shutters, benches, shelves, awnings — three or four different ones
- [ ] Paving, kerbs, gutters, the lane (*gang*) between houses

### Geger Menjangan (C)
- [ ] The path up: surface, steps, vegetation
- [ ] The view from the top, towards town, at two times of day
- [ ] One panorama if the phone does it

### Ordinary textures (A — cheap, do them wherever you are)
- [ ] Walls: plaster, brick, painted, peeling
- [ ] Roof tiles (*genteng*) from below and at an angle
- [ ] Earth: dry cracked soil, wet soil, a hoed row
- [ ] Grass and weeds at ankle height
- [ ] Wood grain on old posts; bamboo up close
- [ ] Stone: river stone, a stone-lined channel

## Deliverable

One GitHub issue, `M1-12 reference photos`, linking the shared folder, with the date and a
line per set (place, time, weather). Tick **M1-12** in the ROADMAP when section A is done;
B and C can follow as separate uploads.
