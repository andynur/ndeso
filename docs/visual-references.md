## How to use this page

The placeholder boxes (M1-09) and the first art pass should get the **materials,
proportions and light** right, not the exact buildings. For each area this page gives:
what to look at, what the look should be (our reading of those sources), and what only a
visit can settle.

## Balé — Mbah Hita's ground (`bale`)

**Look at:** the public Instagram of [Balle Al Jannah](https://www.instagram.com/balle_aljannah/)
and [Masjid Ar Royyan](https://www.instagram.com/masjid_ar_royyan_purworejo/). This is for
atmosphere only. The in-game place is Mbah Hita's own and is **not** a copy of that
venue (round 00, R1).

**The look:**
- A **joglo** pendopo: four *saka guru* holding a raised centre roof, *sunduk* tie-beams,
  and a *blandar* frame. Around it, family buildings with *limasan* and *kampung* roofs.
  [joglo — Dinas Kebudayaan DIY](https://budaya.jogjaprov.go.id/artikel/detail/Mengenal-Bangunan-Berarsitektur-Tradisional-Jawa-Bangunan-Joglo)
  The half-built state should show bare rafters over a finished stone platform, as the
  placeholder already does.
- Terracotta *genteng* tiles that have weathered dark, a whitewashed or bare brick
  *pawon*, a hedge fence (teh-tehan) or a bamboo one.
- A stone-lined **kalen** that is dry and cracked with weeds in its bed (the M3 story
  refills it), and a plank *wot* across it.
- It is inside a town: roofs of houses, a mosque dome or minaret, and power lines at the
  edge of the frame, not endless open countryside.

**Only a visit settles:** the real ground plan, the trees, and whether there is a kalen
at all.

## Pasar Baledono (`pasar`)

**Look at:** [Pasar Baledono before Ramadan 2023 — YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PWT4A2rGuzs) ·
[Purworejo Kita — video from another angle](https://www.facebook.com/purworejo/videos/pasar-baledono-dari-sudut-berbeda-pasar-baledono-tradisional-kakilima-purworejo/1148464350313963/) ·
[SNI certificate article](https://dinkukmp.purworejokab.go.id/pasar-baledono-purworejo-raih-sertifikat-sni-pasar-rakyat-) ·
[rebuild — KRjogja](https://www.krjogja.com/kedu/1242625021/pasar-baledono-mulai-dibangun-pedagang-lega)

**The look:**
- A **modern two-storey market building** (rebuilt after a fire; a musala and offices on
  the upper floor) inside which the old life carries on. Tiled floors and concrete columns,
  with tarpaulins, *tampah*, baskets, crates and hanging scales in front of them.
- **Kakilima and bakul at the edges and on the street in the dark before dawn.** Lamps
  at 04:30, the bustle strongest around 06:00, the produce aisles thinning by late morning.
- Next door, the red **Klenteng Thong Hwie Kiong**
  ([Kekunaan](http://kekunaan.blogspot.com/2012/09/klenteng-thong-hwie-kiong.html) ·
  [Jejak Kolonial](https://jejakkolonial.blogspot.com/2016/04/menyusuri-peninggalan-budaya-tionghoa.html)):
  red and gold, a curved roof ridge, and incense smoke. It should read as a landmark,
  never as a shop.

**Only a visit settles:** which side of the building faces the road, the stall layout,
and what the signs actually look like.

## Jalan Raya (`jalan`)

**Look at:** the first-person drive in the video linked for the pasar (the road
leading to it) and [Purworejo Kita posts](https://www.facebook.com/purworejo/posts/menuju-pasar-baledonojalan-ahmad-yani-purworejo-pasar-baledono-purworejo/719661533759727/).

**The look:** a two-lane asphalt road with open drains (*got*) and low kerbs. Shophouses
(*ruko*) have folding steel shutters, and warung have benches, jars of *kerupuk* and a
glass cabinet. After rain the asphalt shines and neon and LED warung light reflects off
it. The in-game supermarket is fictional (PLACES §1, private-business tier).

## Geger Menjangan (`bukit`)

**Look at:** [NativeIndonesia](https://nativeindonesia.com/geger-menjangan/) ·
[Jogja Super](https://www.jogjasuper.co.id/purworejo/wisata/geger-menjangan/) ·
[Kompasiana — to the top](https://www.kompasiana.com/sobondeso/5500e547a33311376f51279d/puncak-geger-menjangan-purworejo) ·
[YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=zOwV0TLyVrk)

**The look:** a city park with a playground and pool at the foot, and a path climbing
through trees to a **lookout tower** at about 175 m. From the top: the roofs of the town,
the green of the rice fields, the line of the **Bogowonto**, the **Menoreh** hills to the
north, and on a clear day a thin band of the southern sea. The makam of Kyai Imam Puro is
drawn quietly, with no highlighting and no interaction (PLACES §3).

## Kaligesing (`kaligesing`)

**Look at:** [Kaligesing goats — Dinperkimtan](https://dinperkimtan.purworejokab.go.id/pemerintah-kabupaten-purworejo-dukung-pengembangan-kambing-kaligesing) ·
[agrotourism in Kaligono (Dewi Kano)](https://www.researchgate.net/publication/330472830_Strategi_Pengembangan_Agrowisata_dalam_Mendukung_Pembangunan_Pertanian_-_Studi_Kasus_di_Desa_Wisata_Kaligono_Dewi_Kano_Kecamatan_Kaligesing_Kabupaten_Purworejo)

**The look:** hills with tall durian and mangosteen trees and coffee in the understorey.
Kaligesing goats are long-eared, with a white coat and a black head. Their pens are
raised off the ground on posts, with slatted wooden floors.

## Dolalak (festival, M4)

**Look at:** [Kompas — history, costume, movement](https://regional.kompas.com/read/2022/12/09/175349878/tari-dolalak-sejarah-fungsi-busana-dan-gerakan?page=all) ·
[Dindikbud Purworejo performance, 2024](https://dindikbud.purworejokab.go.id/meriahkan-peringatan-hut-ke79-kemerdekaan-ri-dindikbud-gelar-pentas-kesenian-dolalak-di-amphitheater-alun-alun-purworejo)

**The look:** a black shirt with shoulder epaulettes copied from colonial uniforms, dark
glasses, a hat, a *sampur*, and music from *terbang* and *bedug*. See round 00, R5, on the
costume and the trance.

## Light (DESIGN §1.3) — cross-checked against these sources

- **Dawn at the pasar:** shops lit by lamps against a sky still deep blue. The
  `subuh → dawn` keyframes should keep the pasar lamps on until around 06:00.
- **Maghrib:** orange light from the west, set against the Menoreh hills, which are dark
  and north of the town. The camera's default northward view puts the hills in shadow
  and the sky behind them in pink.
- **After rain:** reflections on the asphalt and a green that goes deeper. Rain in M2 should
  raise ground specular on *Jalan Raya* only, not on the field.
